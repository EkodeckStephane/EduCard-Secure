# Legal Validation Required

EduCard Secure is a technical prototype. The following points require human legal validation before real deployment:

- Lawful basis for processing student data.
- Processing involving minors.
- Retention and deletion rules.
- Export and data subject access workflow.
- Incident and breach notification obligations.
- Any biometric processing.
- Data sharing with institutions or service providers.
- Backup location, encryption and restoration procedures.
- Audit log retention and access rights.
- Production use of QR identifiers and verification logs.
- Cross-border transfers if any.
- Processor and subcontractor agreements.
- Authority, parent or guardian notification rules.
- Rules for deleting, archiving or preserving school records.
- Conditions for using exports in disciplinary, financial or administrative decisions.

## Explicitly Not Decided By The Prototype

- Whether a real institution may deploy the system.
- Whether a specific data field is legally mandatory.
- Whether a retention period is lawful.
- Whether biometric processing is permissible.
- Whether backup storage location is acceptable.
